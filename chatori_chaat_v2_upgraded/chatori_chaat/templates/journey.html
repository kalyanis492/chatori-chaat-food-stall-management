Phase 1 – Dream & Planning

Where It All Began

Every great journey starts with a dream. For Piyush and Parnika Patil, the dream was simple yet powerful — to bring authentic Nashik chaat to their neighbourhood, served fresh, hygienic, and with a lot of love.

Months of planning, recipe testing, tasting sessions with family, and learning the art of running a food business — all led to one beautiful beginning.
